Random matchmaking may offer a completed human performance when no new paired
challenge is available. A `human` match copies the original player's own seven
answers and scores, with the same questions and answer order. It does not copy
the original bot opponent's scores, even if that earlier game was against a bot.

These are saved-round comparisons, not new challenges sent to that player.
Direct challenges use their own shared duel and settle after both players finish.
The native apps label saved-round intros, results and history explicitly. From
a saved-round result, the action to play a new game is labelled "Challenge".

Player names and portraits remain linked to their real account. Bots use their
separate name list and have no real-player opponent ID. No player match data or
scores are altered by the wording change.
