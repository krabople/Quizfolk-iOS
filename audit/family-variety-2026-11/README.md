# Family Trivia variety expansion

Batch identifier: `2026-11-family-variety-v1`.

Target: 359 new questions, taking Family Trivia (category 37) from 359 to 718.
Completed and publicly verified live on 18 November 2026; see `deployment.json`.
All additions must be suitable for children and adults playing together. No
Harry Potter or Narnia questions are permitted in this batch. Original questions
are preserved. Drafts are not approved or live until the recorded individual
review and publication checks are complete.

Every published addition carries this batch identifier in `auditVersion` and has
a stable question ID. `added-question-ids.json` will enumerate precisely the
published additions. `baseline.json` records the original category IDs and
fingerprints. To remove this expansion later, use the explicit ID list and batch
identifier together, preserving original questions and subsequent additions.

Author review must examine each question, all four options, the sole correct
offered answer, grammar, source relevance and family suitability. Author review
is not described as independent review. Technical validators are additional
safeguards and do not replace individual judgment.
