Quizfolk privacy

Updated 7 November 2026.

Quizfolk is an independent trivia game for iPhone, iPad and Android. You do not need an email address to play. Optional rewarded advertising is being introduced on iPhone and iPad; the current Android version does not include advertising.

Offline practice

Your chosen interests, display name, practice results and local statistics are stored on your device. Offline results are not submitted to the online league.

Online duels and leagues

When you open the app with a connection, the service creates a random player identifier and a secret access key. On iPhone the key stays in Keychain. On Android it is encrypted with a non-exportable key held in Android Keystore; credentials are excluded from app backup and device transfer. The service stores your display name, match questions, selected answers, answer times, points and results. Your display name and online scores appear in leagues and recorded opponent runs. Do not put information you want to keep private in your display name.

The service uses short-lived, hashed network-address rate-limit records to reduce abuse. The hosting provider may process ordinary access and error logs to operate the service. Optional advertising on supported versions is described below.

Players, follows and challenges

Your player card shows your chosen name, picture, followed topics, topic levels, earned titles, achievements and completed online statistics. Other players can find your name, follow you and send a challenge. In your public card settings you can hide yourself from discovery and pause direct challenges. Following someone makes their completed duel activity visible in your feed. We store follow/block relationships, challenge invitations, participants, shared questions, your answers and results, and the head-to-head record derived from completed human meetings. Challenges can be played at different times; invitations expire after 14 days. Player cards do not ask for your age, gender, precise location, contact list or private messages. We do not send your player card, name, photo, quiz answers or follow relationships to advertising providers. Blocking removes follow links and cancels unfinished challenges between the two players.

Matchmaking uses a short-lived search ticket and a few ordinary HTTPS checks over six seconds. Search tickets do not indicate live presence. A human opponent may be another searcher or a clearly labelled recorded run. Bots are explicitly labelled.

Tournaments

Survival tournaments store your coin entries, coin ledger, question order, run progress, score and correct-answer streak. Your best score, player card name and picture, rank and any prize badge are visible in public tournament standings. Coins are earned game credits with no cash value. Each survival run ends on the first wrong answer, timeout, manual end, full topic completion or event closure. Private friends tournaments store the host settings, invitation code, members, home topics, fixtures and results. Only members can view their standings and fixtures; someone with the invitation code can preview the tournament name, entry cost, status and member count before joining. Private tournaments may be free without prizes or require a coin entry with prizes. Power-up purchases and use are recorded with your match or tournament run. Account deletion removes your runs, wallet, ledger and badge records and anonymises your participation in other players’ saved match results.

Profile pictures

You can choose one photo using the system photo picker on iPhone or Android, without granting access to your whole photo library. The app crops it to a small square and re-encodes it without photo metadata before upload. Your profile picture is stored with your player account and is visible to other players in duels and leagues. You can replace or remove it in You; deleting your account also removes it. Choose a picture you are happy to share with other players.

Optional push notifications

Push notifications start switched off. You can choose new challenges, completed duel results, final tournament prizes and friends-tournament updates independently in You > Notifications. If you enable notifications and grant device permission, we store an installation identifier, a push delivery token and your choices. Apple Push Notification service delivers iPhone notifications; Firebase Cloud Messaging, operated by Google, delivers Android notifications. Push tokens and event messages are used only to deliver the alerts you select. We do not include quiz answers in notification messages, use notification data for advertising, or add Firebase Analytics. Switching a notification category off stops new messages and cancels queued messages in that category. Deleting your account removes its push tokens and notification settings. Delivery records expire after seven days.

Optional rewarded ads and privacy choices

On supported iPhone and iPad versions, you can choose Watch an ad in You > Earn coins to receive 5 coins after completing an ad. Watching is optional; you can continue playing without it. There is no daily reward limit. An ad may be unavailable, and leaving before completion does not earn a reward.

Google AdMob supplies these ads. Google's User Messaging Platform presents applicable privacy choices before ads are requested. You can revisit them through Ad privacy choices in Earn coins when that option applies in your region. Quizfolk requests non-personalised ads, disables Google's publisher first-party identifier and does not request permission to use Apple's advertising identifier. Non-personalised advertising still processes data to deliver ads, measure performance and prevent fraud.

Google's advertising and consent software may process your IP address and approximate location derived from it, device or app identifiers, ad views and interactions, crash reports, and performance information. Quizfolk disables the ads SDK's location-services collection and does not request location access. We do not add Firebase Analytics. Google handles advertising data under its Privacy Policy; see also how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

To verify rewards, our server creates a random, single-use reward reference and the app sends that reference to AdMob. We do not send your Quizfolk access key or player identifier. Google returns a signed completion record containing the reference, ad unit, reward, time and transaction identifier. Our server verifies it before adding coins, and stores the attempt, outcome and coin-ledger entry with your account. This prevents a repeated callback from granting the same reward twice. An interrupted connection may delay confirmation until you next connect.

Deleting your online account removes its reward attempts and wallet entries. A one-way hash of a provider transaction identifier may remain without any player association to prevent replay. Ordinary hosting access logs may contain callback parameters. Google's own retention and deletion practices are described in the policies linked above.

Retention and deletion

Your online account and match history are kept until you delete your account. Open You in the app, connect your player card if necessary, then choose Delete online account. This removes the account’s server history and reports, and anonymises references in other players’ saved opponent runs. Local practice history remains on your device. Account recovery and cross-device sign-in are not available in this initial version.

For a privacy request or bug report, use the project’s issue tracker. Do not post access keys or other private information in a public issue.
