Quizfolk privacy

Updated 26 October 2026.

Quizfolk is an independent iPhone trivia game. It does not use advertising trackers, sell your personal information, or require an email address to play.

Offline practice

Your chosen interests, display name, practice results and local statistics are stored on your device. Offline results are not submitted to the online league.

Online duels and leagues

When you play online or open a league, the service creates a random player identifier and a secret access key. The key stays in your iPhone’s Keychain. The service stores your display name, match questions, selected answers, answer times, points and results. Your display name and online scores appear in leagues and recorded opponent runs. Do not put information you want to keep private in your display name.

The service uses short-lived, hashed network-address rate-limit records to reduce abuse. The hosting provider may process ordinary access and error logs to operate the service. There is no advertising or cross-app tracking.

Retention and deletion

Your online account and match history are kept until you delete your account. Open You in the app, connect your player card if necessary, then choose Delete online account. This removes the account’s server history and reports, and anonymises references in other players’ saved opponent runs. Local practice history remains on your device. Account recovery and cross-device sign-in are not available in this initial version.

For a privacy request or bug report, use the project’s issue tracker. Do not post access keys or other private information in a public issue.
