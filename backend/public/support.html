Report a problem

Tell us what happened and what you expected. These details help us investigate:

  • Your device model and the Quizfolk version you use.
  • The topic, tournament or screen involved.
  • Any error message, and a screenshot if you have one.
  • For a question correction, the question and the answer you think is right.

Please do not send passwords, access keys or other private credentials. Your player card name is enough to begin looking into an account issue.

Your player account

In the app, open You to change your player name or picture and choose your notifications. To remove your online player account, connect to the internet and choose Delete online account in You.

This removes your server account and history. Local practice history remains on your device. Account recovery and signing into the same player account on another device are not currently available.

Privacy questions

Email admin@ways2earn.com for a privacy or deletion request. Read the Quizfolk privacy policy for details of the information the app uses.
