Quizfolk's question bank has been individually reviewed and edited for meaning, accuracy, grammar, answer uniqueness and topic relevance. The published bank contains 21,667 accepted questions. Editing does not imply endorsement by the original contributors.

Adapted question data

The bank includes material adapted from Open Trivia Database community contributors and OpenTriviaQA contributors, licensed under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International. The adapted question bank is distributed under the same licence. No endorsement is implied.

Changes include individual editorial corrections to questions and answers, grammar and typography changes, topic reclassification, removal of unsuitable and duplicate records, HTML entity decoding, conversion to JSON/SQLite, stable identifiers and shuffled answer order in play. Editorial review does not guarantee that no errors remain.

The original database snapshot was retrieved through open-trivia-dataset, revision 77d8e3c. The additional bank came from OpenTriviaQA, revision dcc1cdf3.

The current adapted question bank and full notices are available in the public project repository.
